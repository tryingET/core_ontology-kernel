# UFO situation (core.UfoCategory.Situation)

## Definition
The UFO category of states of affairs: portions of reality, made of other entities, that can be comprehended as a whole.

## Typical usage
- Test question: Does it hold or not hold at a given time?
- Governance-core concepts in this category: core.Driver, core.Exception, core.Relief, core.Waiver.
