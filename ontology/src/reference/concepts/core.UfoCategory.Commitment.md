# UFO commitment (core.UfoCategory.Commitment)

## Definition
The UFO-C category of social moments by which one agent is bound towards another to bring about a propositional content; each is paired with a claim.

## Typical usage
- Test question: Who is bound, towards whom, to bring about what?
- Governance-core concepts in this category: core.CarriedObligation, core.TaskClaim.
