# Rückmeldung (core.Rueckmeldung)

## Definition
What Leitung returns: actual results and review findings, measured against the Vorgabe, that feed the next round of Führung.

## Typical usage
- Deviations reported as Rückmeldung become new drivers (Act 2 → Act 1).

## Common confusions
- Not any feedback: a Rückmeldung closes one level's loop and feeds its Führung.
